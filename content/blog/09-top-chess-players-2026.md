**Magnus Carlsen is the world's highest-rated classical player, and Gukesh Dommaraju is the World Champion.** They are different people, because a rating and a title measure different things. The table below is the FIDE Open list of 1 August 2026; newer lists have been published since, and the changes we could verify are listed underneath.

## What has changed since August

- **September 2026:** R Praggnanandhaa rose to 2761 and became India's top-rated player, ranked 8th, ahead of Erigaisi on 2759 (Chess.com and ChessBase India reports of the FIDE list). Read the full story in [India's highest-rated chess player](/blog/india-highest-rated-chess-player).
- **October 2026:** the Wikipedia pages of Praggnanandhaa and Erigaisi, which cite FIDE, show them at 2763 (world No. 7) and 2746 (world No. 12). We have not read the October list itself, so treat these as unconfirmed.

## Rating versus title

FIDE's rating list is recalculated every month from rated games using the [Elo system](/blog/chess-rating-elo-explained). The World Championship is a separate title decided by a qualification cycle and a match. Gukesh won it in December 2024 by beating Ding Liren, and he defends it against Sindarov in Geneva this November. See the [match guide](/blog/world-chess-championship-2026-preview). His classical rating on the August list, 2703, ranked him second among juniors rather than near the top of the Open list.

## The women's top 5 (August 2026)

Hou Yifan 2596, Lei Tingjie 2561, Ju Wenjun 2553, Zhu Jiner 2550 and Bibisara Assaubayeva 2538. Three Indian women appear in the top 15: Koneru Humpy (8th, 2524), Divya Deshmukh (13th, 2490) and Vaishali Rameshbabu (14th, 2489).

## India as a chess nation

On FIDE's federation ranking of 1 August 2026, which averages each country's top 10 players, India was second in the Open section (2705) behind the United States (2723). Indian players in the Open top 15 that month were Erigaisi (9th), Praggnanandhaa (12th) and Viswanathan Anand (14th, 2739).

## Frequently asked questions

**Who is the best chess player in the world right now?**
By classical FIDE rating, Magnus Carlsen, who was on 2823 on the August 2026 list. By title, Gukesh Dommaraju is World Champion.

**Is the highest-rated player the World Champion?**
No. The title is won in a match, and the rating list ranks players by their results over many games.

**How often do rankings change?**
FIDE publishes a new list monthly, so check the date on any ranking you read.
