**The 2026 World Chess Championship match is Gukesh Dommaraju against challenger Javokhir Sindarov, played in Geneva, Switzerland.** FIDE announced Geneva as the host city in July 2026. Both players are 20 years old, according to the FIDE announcement.

## Dates

World Chess reported that FIDE changed the schedule after the first announcement. The revised schedule it reports is:

- A match period running from **22 November to 13 December**.
- **Game 1 on 24 November.**
- The last scheduled classical game (Game 14) on **11 December**, with tie-breaks on **12 December** if the score is level.

These dates were changed once already, so treat them as reported rather than final and confirm the schedule on the [FIDE announcement](https://www.fide.com/geneva-to-host-fide-world-championship-match-2026/) before planning to watch.

## Format

The championship match is played over 14 classical games. The first player to reach 7.5 points wins. If the score is 7-7 after 14 games, rapid and then blitz tie-breaks decide the title. This is the format used in recent championship matches; read the official regulations for any change this year.

## Why this match matters

Gukesh won the title in December 2024 by beating Ding Liren, becoming the youngest World Champion in history. This is his first title defence. For background on his rating compared with other Indian players, see [India's highest-rated chess players](/blog/india-highest-rated-chess-player).
